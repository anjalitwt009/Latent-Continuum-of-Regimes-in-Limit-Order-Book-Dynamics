# Unimodality certificate calibration

The dip criterion was calibrated over ten simulated replications of a genuine
continuum and a discrete three-cluster alternative.

| Simulated structure | Replications | Mean dip | Range | Dip criterion result |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| Genuine continuum | 10 | 0.00107 | [0.00080, 0.00137] | Passed in 10/10 |
| Discrete three-cluster alternative | 10 | 0.12527 | [0.12308, 0.12664] | Failed in 10/10 |

The calibration identifies the dip certificate as the primary discriminator
for this alternative. Effective dimension and the tested H0/H1 summaries did
not independently distinguish the two simulated structures; they remain
supporting diagnostics for the empirical state cloud.
